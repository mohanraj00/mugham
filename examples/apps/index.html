Mugham example apps

All data is synthetic.

  • Support desk

    A ticket queue and a thread with a customer, a person on the team and two agents. An agent drafts a reply, and a person corrects it and sends it.

  • Change review

    An agent proposes a timetable change with a citation and source excerpts. A reviewer approves or rejects it with a confirm step.

  • Field inspection

    A registered checklist component in a thread, for a phone. An inspector saves the findings and closes the inspection.
